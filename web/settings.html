Watch settings手表设置

Set up the voice notes over a USB cable: Wi-Fi, the OpenAI key, your own words, and look at your notes. This page has no server: what you type goes from your computer straight to the watch, nowhere else. 用 USB 线设置手表的速记功能：Wi-Fi、OpenAI key、个人词表，还能查看和导出你的速记。 这个网页没有服务器：你输入的内容只会从你的电脑直接发到手表上，不会经过任何其他地方。

1Connect the watch连接手表

Chrome or Edge on a computer, the watch on a USB data cable. It restarts once when it connects; that is normal. 需要电脑上的 Chrome 或 Edge 浏览器，并用能传数据的 USB 线连接手表。连接时手表会重启一次，这是正常的。

2Wi-Fi

The watch goes online with these to send your notes. Up to five; the newest is tried first. 2.4 GHz networks only. 手表用这些网络上传速记，最多保存 5 个，最新加的优先尝试。只支持 2.4 GHz 网络。

    3OpenAI keyOpenAI key

    Your notes are transcribed and tidied with your own key. Best a key of its own project, with only the permissions it needs and a monthly spend limit. The watch never gives the key back: it shows only the last four characters. 速记用你自己的 key 转录和整理。建议为手表单独建一个项目 key，只开放需要的权限，并设置每月消费上限。 手表不会把 key 传回来，只显示最后 4 位。
