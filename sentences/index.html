Sentence book句子本

Your own sentences, from a podcast or an article, for the watch's sentence book: the meaning on the first side, the German on the second, the pattern on the third. Nothing leaves your computer but for the watch. 把你从听力、文章里总结的句子放进手表的「句子本」：第一面是中文（或英文），第二面是德语，第三面是句式。 内容只会从你的电脑发到手表，不经过任何其他地方。

Connect the watch连接手表

Chrome or Edge on a computer, a USB data cable. The watch restarts once when it connects. 需要电脑上的 Chrome 或 Edge，用能传数据的 USB 线连接。连接时手表会重启一次。

Paste a whole exercise: each sentence as its meaning, then the German, then (if there is one) the pattern, one per line. You see them before they are saved. 把整组练习直接粘贴进来：每句依次是中文、德语、句式（如果有），各占一行。像「场景练习：…」这样的行会当作主题， 「练习 1」「收起」这类会被跳过。保存之前你会先看到拆分结果。
